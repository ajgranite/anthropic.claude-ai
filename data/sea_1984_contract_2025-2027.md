## QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE

### WAGES (Article XIX)

#### Current Status
- **FY 2026 (July 1, 2025):** Current wages remain in effect (no increase specified)
- **FY 2027:** Reopener no later than January 30, 2026 for wages ONLY
- If no agreement on wages, current wages remain for full term

#### Step Structure (11 steps + Trainee + Advancement)
| Step Range | Time Between Steps |
|------------|-------------------|
| Steps 1-7 | 1 year each |
| Steps 7-11 | 2 years each |
| Step T (Trainee) | Below Step 1 until meets minimum requirements |
| Step A (Advancement) | Above Step 11 for in-band advancement |

#### Promotion Pay
- Move to lowest step that increases hourly rate by:
  - At least one step from former pay band, OR
  - At least 3% if at step 11 in former pay band

#### In-Band Advancement
- Employee may request (Per 303.01/303.02 procedure)
- Results in pay at next higher step within pay band
- Can be removed if qualifications no longer met or duties no longer required

### WORK SCHEDULES (Article VI)

| Employee Type | Basic Work Week/Period |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Clerical, Supervisory, Professional | 37.5 hours/week |
| Trade, Custodial | 40 or 37.5 hours/week |
| Law Enforcement | 160 hours/28 days |
| Fire Protection | 212 hours/28 days |

- **Rest Periods:** 15 minutes per 4 hours worked
- **Meal Period:** 30 min to 1 hour (unpaid)
- **Schedule Changes:** 3 calendar weeks notice required

---

### OVERTIME (Article VII)

#### Non-Exempt Employees
| Work Week | First 2.5 hrs OT | Beyond 2.5 hrs OT |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| 37.5 hours | Straight time | Time and one-half |
| 40 hours | Time and one-half | Time and one-half |

#### Exempt Employees
- Compensatory time OR straight time pay at employer discretion

#### Call Back (7.3.1)
- **Minimum:** 3 hours at 1.5x rate
- Portal-to-portal for full-time employees
- No additional 3-hour minimum if called again within initial 3-hour period

#### On-Call (7.3.2)
- **Pay:** 1 hour for every 4 hours on-call
- If actually called back: Call Back provisions apply

#### Standby (7.3.3)
- Must be available for immediate return
- Counted as time worked for regular and OT compensation

#### Compensatory Time Limits
- Cannot be required to accrue more than one basic workweek
- Must be used within 1 year or paid out
- Paid at rate at time of payment

### ANNUAL LEAVE (Article X)

| Years of Service | Days/Month | Days/Year | Maximum Accrual |
|------------------|------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 0-1 | 1 | 12 | 12* |
| 2-8 | 1.25 | 15 | 32 |
| 9-15 | 1.5 | 18 | 38 |
| 16-20 | 1.75 | 21 | 44 |
| 21+ | 2 | 24 | 50 |

*No payment for unused leave upon separation in first 12 months

**New Employee Benefit:** First 5 days immediately available on first day (no additional accrual for first 5 months)

**Day Equivalents:**
- 37.5 hr week: 1 day = 7.5 hrs
- 40 hr week: 1 day = 8 hrs

---

### SICK LEAVE (Article XI)

| Years of Service | Days/Month | Days/Year | Maximum Accrual |
|------------------|------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 0-8 | 1.25 | 15 | 90 |
| 9-15 | 1.25 | 15 | 105 |
| 16+ | 1.25 | 15 | 120 |

#### Allowable Uses (11.2)
- Illness, injury, exposure to contagious disease
- Medical/dental appointments (with prior approval)
- Death in family (bereavement)
- Care for ill/injured minor child
- Care for adult family member incapable of self-care (FMLA)
- **Wellness:** Up to 2 days/fiscal year without medical justification

#### Bereavement Leave (11.2.1)
- Up to 5 days sick leave for death in family
- Not counted against bonus leave accumulation

#### Family Definition (11.2.2)
Spouse, children, minor/dependent children of spouse, mother-in-law, father-in-law, parents, step-parents, step-children, step-brother, step-sister, foster child, grandparents, grandchildren, brothers, sisters, legal guardian, daughter-in-law, son-in-law (may be expanded at supervisor discretion)

#### Parental Leave (11.2.3)
- Up to 12 weeks of any available paid leave
- For birth or adoption
- Must be taken within 12 months
- Counts as FMLA entitlement

#### Sick Leave Payout at Retirement
- 50% of remaining days (max 60 days eligible = max 30 days payout)
- Only for RSA 100-A:5 or 100-A:6 retirement

### GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE (Article XIV)

| Step | Level | Time to Respond |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| I | Supervisor/HR | 10 working days |
| II | Agency Head/Manager of Employee Relations | Meeting within 10 days, response within 10 days after |
| III | Arbitration | 30 days to request |

- **Filing Deadline:** 15 working days from when grievant knew/should have known
- **Arbitrator's Decision:** Final and binding (unless contrary to law or requires additional appropriation)

### INVESTIGATION RIGHTS (Article 16.5)

#### Employer Obligations
- Written notice within 10 business days of investigation commencement
- Bi-weekly status updates
- Complete investigation within 90 days (extensions require Director of Personnel approval)

#### Notice Must Include
- That investigation has commenced
- Alleged conduct/offenses (dates, times, locations unless disclosure interferes)
- Specific policies/laws allegedly violated
- Date investigation commenced

#### If No Discipline Results
- Employee notified in writing
- All reports/documents stored separately from personnel file

---

### LAYOFF PROCEDURES (Article 16.9)

#### Order of Layoff
1. Temporary fill-in employees
2. Initial probationary employees
3. By seniority (less senior first)

#### Notice
- 14 calendar days written notice
- Exception: temp, seasonal, part-time, intermittent

#### Bumping Rights (16.10)
- Available to employees with 10+ years continuous service
- Must notify within 5 working days of layoff notice
- Can bump employee with less than 10 years in lower salary grade position
- Must be certified as qualified for the position

---

### ASSOCIATION RIGHTS (Article III)

#### Stewards
- Number varies by agency (total 94 across all agencies)
- Reasonable time during work hours for representation
- 3 days training per contract year

#### Bargaining
- 11 members on state-level bargaining team
- Preparation time: 1 day/week during even-numbered years starting Sept 1
- Negotiation time: 2 days/week once negotiations begin
- Time spent is considered time worked

#### President's Leave (3.11)
- 2-year paid leave of absence
- Association reimburses full cost of wages and benefits

## NOTABLE PROVISIONS

### Federal Government Shutdown (16.12)
- Positions funded 75%+ by federal source: fully furloughed
- Positions funded less than 75%: partially furloughed
- No loss of rights/benefits except lost pay
- Employer provides unemployment documentation

### Workers Compensation (11.2.4)
- Health/dental benefits continue
- No change to seniority, increment, longevity, leave accrual dates
- Actual leave accrual resumes on return

### Return from Investigation (16.5.1)
- Calculation of lost compensation includes lost overtime at 12-month average

### Footwear Reimbursement (19.6.1)
- Up to $250 per biennium if PPE assessment requires specific footwear

### Mileage (19.4.3)
- IRS maximum rate
- Portal-to-portal on call back

### Meals (19.4.4)
- In-state overnight: GSA per diem for Merrimack County
- Out-of-state: GSA Travel Per Diem Rates
